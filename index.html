Only 19.1% of toddlers met minimum dietary diversity in 2025–26 — fewer than one in five, and down 16 points from 2020 on the same definition [1][4].

2025–262016

Diarrhoea raised the odds of stunting by 24% and fever by 19% in the 2020 survey model [4]. In 2016, 74% of children with diarrhoea were given less food than usual [5].

    What works — effect sizes

    Reduction in risk, % · Cochrane, Lancet, IPD meta-analyses [40][46][125][126][127]
    No nutrition trial has ever been run in Timor-Leste. The only cluster-RCT (WASH-for-WORMS) found no child-health gain beyond deworming; the MDG-F evaluation improved wasting severity but not stunting [41][42].

    Investment case

    World Bank frameworks [38][39][129]

    What does not work alone

    Randomised and meta-analytic evidence [42][62][63][102][103][104]

    WASH hardware. WASH Benefits (Bangladesh) and SHINE (Zimbabwe) found no stunting effect from household WASH despite cutting diarrhoea; Timor-Leste's own trial was null too. WASH remains essential for health — but should not promise growth [42][62][63].

    Unconditional cash alone. Meta-analyses find height-for-age gains of ≈0–0.03 SD. Cash is a platform; its nutrition payoff depends on conditionality and bundled services [102][103][104].
